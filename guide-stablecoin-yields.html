Lending yield is not a fast way to make money — it's a fine way to not lose money to inflation on capital that would otherwise sit idle. The two are frequently conflated in "passive income" content, and the conflation is where people get disappointed.

What actually carries risk here

  • Smart-contract risk — Aave and Compound are both heavily audited and have run for years without a loss-causing exploit at the core protocol level, but "heavily audited" is not "zero risk."
  • Stablecoin depeg risk — you're exposed to whichever stablecoin you lend, not just the protocol.
  • Rate volatility — APY floats with utilization; the number today is not a forward guarantee.
